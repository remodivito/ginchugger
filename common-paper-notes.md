# Common Paper: annotated rationale and market figures (notes)

Read 14 October 2026 for grounding the two-sided playbooks (decision 148). Two sources, both Common Paper's own pages; quotations are theirs.

## Cloud Service Agreement v2.1, annotated page
https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/ (CC BY 4.0: "Common Paper agreements are free to use and modify under CC BY 4.0")

- General cap: default a multiple of fees; "85% of CSAs use this option, and most set it to 1x". "Paid or payable" wording explained (paid-only lets a customer shrink exposure by not paying). A $0 cap "would be unenforceable".
- Increased cap: default Increased Claims are privacy/security and confidentiality; "the trend in SaaS companies has been to use an Increased Cap Amount, or supercap"; "only 13% of CSAs include Increased Claims".
- Unlimited claims: default is the indemnity obligation; "about 1% of CSAs include Unlimited Claims"; "fully uncapped and unlimited claims has come to be seen as a risk that is hard to justify".
- Indemnities: provider covers IP infringement from the service, customer covers Customer Content and breach of Restrictions; 76% keep each default; indemnity commonly the "sole and exclusive remedy"; first-party claims excluded.
- Usage data: provider may use to maintain and improve; disclose only aggregated, non-identifying.
- Feedback: no assignment, because "many customers bristle at seeing an assignment".
- Machine learning: "the Committee opted to include a default clause to permit basic training of AI and machine learning models", with aggregation and de-identification; removable via Other Changes.
- Suspension: three grounds (undisputed balance over 30 days, breach of Restrictions, material harm), with or without notice; "a fairly reasonable risk allocation because it is not permanent termination".
- Payment: 30 days from receipt in 62% of CSAs; monthly 48% / annual 46% invoicing; no late fees by design ("payment disputes were more often an oversight by the customer").
- Renewal: 90% auto-renew; of those 84% need 30 days' notice; 29% allow increases on renewal, "typically 5–8%".
- Termination: no termination for convenience ("uncommon in SaaS subscription agreements"); for cause with 30-day cure; prorated refunds only on listed triggers.
- Data: deletion within 60 days of request; backup and legal retention excepted. No rationale for the timelines.
- Warranty: no material reduction in functionality; 45 days' notice, 45 days to restore, then terminate with prorated refund; exclusive remedy.
- Changes: in writing, signed or accepted by both; versions never change once posted.
- Assignment: either party on notice after merger/change of control/asset sale; otherwise consent.
- Governing law: left blank; 71% Delaware. Publicity: name and logo on a customer list by default; "some customers prefer to remove this grant".
- Not on the page: audit rights (none in the CSA); which side pushes back, for most clauses; benchmarks for confidentiality, suspension, warranty, deletion, assignment.

## 2026 SaaS contract benchmark report
https://commonpaper.com/resources/2026-saas-contract-benchmark-report/ ("2026 © Common Paper. All rights reserved": quotable facts, not a licensed dataset)

16,140 signed agreements, 2,223 companies; CSA, Mutual NDA, Design Partner; "2026" = June 2025 to June 2026.
- Liability: 99% cap as a multiple of fees, 96% at 1x; 0.5x fell 7% → 2.5% (2024 → 2026); supercaps 4.1% → 6.9%; 2x supercap 0.3% → 2.7%; unlimited claims 1.1% → 10.2% (gross negligence, wilful misconduct, fraud); over half of increased-claim agreements removed a default item.
- Insurance required: 11% → 13%; cyber 9% → 12%.
- AI: any AI language 38% → 53%; AI-specific negotiated edit about 20% of CSAs; prohibiting AI training <1% → 11% → 14%; vendor training rights granted about 3%.
- SLAs 29% → 26%; security policy 37% → 34%, "reasonable efforts" 90%+ of those.
- Delaware 83% → 74%; auto-renewal 85% → 87%; 30-day non-renewal window about 70%; automatic fee increases 23% → 13%; 30-day payment "the norm".
- Mutual NDA: fixed term 71% → 78% (most commonly 1 year); confidentiality period fixed 79% / unlimited 21%, two years 75% of fixed; Delaware 84%.
- Not stated: per-year denominators, confidence intervals, how "negotiated" was defined, deal-size or industry splits, how often either side prevails.
