# The two playbooks, side by side

Generated by two-sided-table.mjs from playbook.json (customer side, v3) and playbook-vendor.json (vendor side, v1). Both take the clean Common Paper CSA v2.1 as compliant; they differ in which direction of change is a departure and how far each side will go. The vendor side's `source` column records the Common Paper rationale or market figure behind the position (see ../common-paper-notes.md).
